About

A small model (Qwen3.5-2B, fine-tuned with SFT and then GRPO) writes the workout; every answer is then checked against the exercise catalog. On 300 test requests the model alone was fully correct 55% of the time, and the full checked system (catalog guard and retries) 82%.
